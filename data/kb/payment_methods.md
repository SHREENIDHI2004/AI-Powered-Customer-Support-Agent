# Payment Methods Policy

## Accepted Payment Options
We accept a variety of secure payment methods for your convenience:
- Major Credit & Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Shop Pay.
- Buy Now, Pay Later: Klarna 4-installment interest-free payment plans for orders between $35 and $1,000.

## Payment Security
All transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL encryption. We do not store full credit card details on our servers.

## Payment Restrictions & Unaccepted Payment Methods
- **Cryptocurrency**: We do NOT accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, or any form of cryptocurrency.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: We do NOT offer Cash on Delivery (COD) services for any orders.
- **Checks and Money Orders**: Personal checks, cashier's checks, and wire transfers are not accepted for online retail purchases.
