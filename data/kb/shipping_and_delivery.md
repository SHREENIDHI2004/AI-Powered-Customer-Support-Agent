# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Shipping Options & Rates
We offer several shipping methods for domestic customers within the continental United States:
- **Standard Shipping (3-5 business days)**: $4.99 or FREE on orders over $50.
- **Express Shipping (2 business days)**: $12.99 flat rate.
- **Overnight Shipping (1 business day)**: $24.99 flat rate (orders must be placed before 12:00 PM EST).

## Order Processing Time
Orders are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours on business days (Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays). Orders placed on weekends will be processed on the next business day.

## Order Tracking
Once your package leaves our fulfillment center, you will receive an automated shipping confirmation email containing your tracking number and carrier link (UPS, FedEx, or USPS).

## International Shipping
We ship internationally to over 40 countries. International shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Delivery times range from 7 to 14 business days. Note that international orders may be subject to local import duties, taxes, and customs clearance fees, which are the responsibility of the customer.
