# Product Specifications: VoltCore Solar Power Bank 20000mAh

## Overview
VoltCore is an outdoor rugged solar power bank built for emergency backup charging, camping, and hiking.

## Key Features
- **Capacity**: 20,000mAh high-density lithium polymer battery (charges smartphones 4 to 5 times).
- **Output Ports**: USB-C Power Delivery 30W bi-directional fast charge port + Dual USB-A Quick Charge 3.0 ports.
- **Solar Charging**: High-efficiency monocrystalline solar panel for trickle emergency outdoor solar charging.
- **Durability**: IP65 waterproof, dustproof, and shock-resistant reinforced rubber housing.
- **Bonus**: Built-in 3-mode LED flashlight (Strong, Soft, SOS Distress strobe).
- **Price**: $49.99
