# Product Specifications: ErgoFlex Ergonomic Office Chair

## Overview
The ErgoFlex Chair is designed to provide full-body support and promote healthy posture during long work or gaming sessions.

## Key Features
- **Lumbar Support**: Dynamic height-adjustable lumbar support cushion.
- **Armrests**: 3D adjustable armrests (height, depth, and angle).
- **Material**: Breathable Korean mesh backrest for airflow and high-density molded foam seat cushion.
- **Recline**: Synchronous tilt mechanism with 90° to 135° tilt locking angles.
- **Weight Capacity**: Tested and certified for up to 300 lbs (136 kg).
- **Price**: $299.99
