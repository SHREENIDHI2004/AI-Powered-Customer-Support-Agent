# Product Specifications: KeyCraft Pro Mechanical Keyboard

## Overview
KeyCraft Pro is a 75% compact wireless mechanical keyboard designed for typists, programmers, and gamers.

## Key Features
- **Switches**: Hot-swappable PCB supporting 3-pin and 5-pin mechanical switches (pre-lubed Gateron Pro Red linear switches included).
- **Keycaps**: Durable double-shot PBT keycaps with Cherry profile.
- **Connectivity**: Tri-mode connection (Bluetooth 5.1, 2.4GHz low-latency wireless dongle, and detachable USB-C wired mode).
- **RGB Lighting**: 19 pre-programmed per-key South-facing RGB lighting modes.
- **Battery**: 4000mAh rechargeable lithium battery providing up to 200 hours of typing with RGB off.
- **Price**: $119.99
