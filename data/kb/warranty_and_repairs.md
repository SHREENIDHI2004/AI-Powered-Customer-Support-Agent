# Warranty & Repairs Policy

## Standard Manufacturer Warranty
All electronic hardware products sold on our store come with a **1-year limited manufacturer warranty** starting from the delivery date.

## Warranty Coverage
The warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship.
- Internal component failure under normal usage conditions.
- Defective buttons, ports, or charging modules.

## Warranty Exclusions
The warranty does NOT cover:
- Accidental drops, physical impact, cosmetic damage, or cracked screens.
- Water or liquid damage unless the product explicitly specifies water submersion protection rating.
- Damage caused by unauthorized modifications, disassembling, or third-party repair attempts.
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., gradual battery degradation over time).

## Submitting a Claim
To file a warranty claim:
1. Contact customer support with your original order receipt.
2. Provide a photo or video demonstration of the issue.
3. If approved, we will issue a prepaid shipping label for repair or send a replacement unit.
