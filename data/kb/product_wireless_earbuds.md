# Product Specifications: AirPulse True Wireless Earbuds

## Overview
AirPulse earbuds offer compact ergonomic fit, sweatproof IPX7 rating, and rich bass performance for daily workouts.

## Key Features
- **Water Resistance**: IPX7 fully waterproof rating (sweatproof and rainproof).
- **Audio**: 10mm dynamic drivers with custom equalizer controls via app.
- **Battery Life**: 6 hours of continuous earbud listening + 24 extra hours from wireless charging case (30 hours total).
- **Controls**: Intuitive touch controls for volume, track skipping, and voice assistant calls.
- **Price**: $69.99
