# Product Specifications: SoundSphere ANC Wireless Headphones

## Overview
SoundSphere Wireless Headphones feature Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) technology and high-fidelity 40mm neodymium drivers for immersive audio experience.

## Key Features
- **Noise Cancellation**: Advanced Hybrid ANC reduces ambient noise by up to 35dB. Transparency Mode lets ambient sounds in with a button click.
- **Battery Life**: 40 hours of continuous playback with ANC ON, up to 60 hours with ANC OFF. Quick 10-minute USB-C charge yields 4 hours of playback.
- **Microphone**: Quad-microphone array with AI ENC environmental noise reduction for crystal clear calls.
- **Comfort**: Ultra-soft memory foam ear cushions with adjustable protein leather headband.
- **Connectivity**: Bluetooth 5.3 multi-point connection (pair up to 2 devices simultaneously) + 3.5mm wired backup jack.
- **Price**: $189.99
