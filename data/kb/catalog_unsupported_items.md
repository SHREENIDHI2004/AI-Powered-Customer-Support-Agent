# Store Catalog Exclusions & Unsupported Products

## Products Not Sold
Our store specializes in personal tech electronics, office ergonomic gear, and lifestyle hydration accessories. We explicitly do NOT carry or sell:
- **Electric Scooters & E-Bikes**: We do not sell electric scooters, e-bikes, or motorized transport vehicles.
- **Smartphones**: We do not sell iPhones, Samsung Galaxy phones, or any mobile handsets directly.
- **Groceries & Fresh Food**: We do not carry perishable food or grocery items.
- **Automotive Parts**: We do not stock car batteries, tires, or auto replacement parts.
