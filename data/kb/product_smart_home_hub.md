# Product Specifications: OmniConnect Smart Home Hub

## Overview
OmniConnect is a universal smart home gateway connecting all smart lighting, security sensors, locks, and thermostats into one unified hub.

## Key Features
- **Protocols Supported**: Matter over Thread, Zigbee 3.0, Z-Wave Plus, Wi-Fi 2.4/5GHz, and Bluetooth Low Energy.
- **Voice Assistant Integration**: Works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit.
- **Local Control**: Processes local automation rules offline without relying on cloud servers.
- **Range**: Covers up to 2,500 sq ft with built-in mesh networking relay.
- **Price**: $89.99
