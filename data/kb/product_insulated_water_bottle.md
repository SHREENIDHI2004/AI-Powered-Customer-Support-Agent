# Product Specifications: HydroShield Insulated Water Bottle

## Overview
HydroShield is a durable 18/8 food-grade stainless steel vacuum-insulated water bottle for everyday hydration and outdoor sports.

## Key Features
- **Capacity**: 32 oz (950 ml).
- **Insulation**: TempTech double-wall vacuum insulation keeps beverages icy cold for 24 hours or piping hot for 12 hours.
- **Lid Options**: Includes 2 leak-proof lids: Straw Lid for easy drinking and Chug Lid for high flow.
- **Safety**: 100% BPA-free, phthalate-free, non-toxic materials. Sweat-proof powder-coated exterior finish.
- **Price**: $24.99
