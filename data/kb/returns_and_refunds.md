# Return & Refund Policy

## Overview
We offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy for most items purchased on our store. The return window starts from the date your order is delivered.

## Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for a full refund:
- Items must be unused, unwashed, and in the original packaging with all tags attached.
- Electronics must include all original cables, manuals, and accessories.
- Proof of purchase (order number or invoice receipt) is required.

## Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned or exchanged:
- Clearance or "Final Sale" items.
- Personal care items, hygiene products, and unsealed innerwear.
- Downloadable software products or digital gift cards.

## Refund Process
Once your returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse (typically 2-3 business days), your refund will be processed automatically to your original payment method. Refunds take 5-10 business days to appear on your bank statement depending on your card issuer. 

## Shipping Fees for Returns
Return shipping is FREE for defective items or order errors made by us. For standard remorse returns (e.g., changed mind or wrong size), a flat return shipping label fee of $5.99 will be deducted from your refund.
