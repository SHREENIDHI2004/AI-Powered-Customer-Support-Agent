# Product Specifications: CineBeam Portable Mini Projector

## Overview
CineBeam is a portable Full HD smart projector featuring built-in streaming apps and battery powered mobility.

## Key Features
- **Resolution**: Native 1080p Full HD (1920x1080) with 4K decoding support.
- **Brightness**: 500 ANSI Lumens for vivid picture quality in low to medium light environments.
- **Screen Size**: Projects display sizes from 40 inches up to 150 inches.
- **Smart Features**: Built-in Android TV OS with pre-installed Netflix, YouTube, and Prime Video. Auto-focus and 4-point Keystone correction.
- **Audio**: Dual 5W Harman Kardon stereo speakers built-in.
- **Price**: $249.99
