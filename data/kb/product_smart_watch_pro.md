# Product Specifications: ApexFit Pro Smartwatch

## Overview
The ApexFit Pro Smartwatch is a premium fitness and health tracking smartwatch designed for active lifestyles.

## Key Features
- **Display**: 1.4-inch Crisp AMOLED touch display (454x454 resolution).
- **Health Sensors**: 24/7 continuous heart rate monitoring, SpO2 blood oxygen tracking, sleep analysis, and stress level tracking.
- **Battery Life**: Up to 7 days of normal use on a single charge; magnetic fast charger included (0 to 100% in 75 minutes).
- **Water Resistance**: 5 ATM water resistance rating (suitable for swimming down to 50 meters).
- **Connectivity**: Bluetooth 5.2, GPS + GLONASS positioning.
- **Compatibility**: Works seamlessly with iOS 14.0+ and Android 8.0+ devices via the free ApexFit Companion app.
- **Price**: $149.99
