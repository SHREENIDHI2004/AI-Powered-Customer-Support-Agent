# Gift Cards & Promotional Codes Policy

## Digital Gift Cards
- Digital gift cards can be purchased in denominations from $10 to $500.
- Gift cards are sent electronically via email immediately upon purchase confirmation.
- Gift cards **never expire** and carry zero inactivity fees.

## Promotional Coupons & Promo Codes
- Only **one promotional code** can be applied per order at checkout. Promo codes cannot be stacked.
- Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively to previously placed orders.
- Promotional discounts do not apply to gift card purchases, taxes, or shipping fees.
