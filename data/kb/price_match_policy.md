# Price Matching Policy

## Overview
We guarantee competitive pricing on all our catalog items. If you find a lower price on an identical item from an authorized US retailer, we will happily match the price at the time of purchase or within 14 days of your order.

## Price Match Requirements
- The product must be identical in brand, model number, color, and size.
- The competitor product must be in stock and available for immediate shipping.
- Proof of the competitor's active price (direct website link or print ad) must be submitted to support.

## Price Match Exclusions
- **Amazon & Third-Party Marketplaces**: We do NOT price match third-party sellers on Amazon, eBay, Walmart Marketplace, or Craigslist. We only match items sold directly by Amazon.com as the seller and shipper (not marketplace sellers).
- Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Flash Sales, clearance sales, and refurbished/used items are excluded from price matching.
- Pricing errors or coupon stack discounts from competitors will not be matched.
