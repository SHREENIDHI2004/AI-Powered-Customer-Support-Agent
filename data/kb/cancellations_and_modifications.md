# Order Cancellations & Modifications

## Order Cancellation Window
You can cancel your order free of charge within **1 hour** of placing it. 

## How to Cancel
1. Log into your store account and navigate to "My Orders".
2. Select your recent order and click "Cancel Order" if available.
3. Alternatively, contact Customer Support immediately via chat or support ticket with your order ID.

## Address & Item Modifications
If you need to change the shipping address or edit items in your order, you must do so within 1 hour of purchase. Once an order enters the automated fulfillment pipeline, we cannot modify the shipping address or add/remove items.

## Cancellations After Dispatch
If your order has already shipped, it cannot be canceled. You may return the item following our Standard Return Policy once it arrives.
